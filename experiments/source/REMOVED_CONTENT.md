# Retained and omitted material

The package retains prepared inputs, original prediction ledgers, the exact mismatch plans and 200 executed mismatch summary rows, current source/J refitting code, and all numerical limitations that constrain the paper.

Large search caches, duplicate old predictions, intermediate logs and unrelated development refitting branches are not included. Their material negative findings remain summarized with numbers in Appendix S13.6 and the compact historical development ledgers. Reproducible 10,000-draw arrays are generated by the statistics script rather than distributed as duplicate bulk data.

V intervals spanning zero, DIE2 sensitivity, the full-context M pair interval spanning zero, both selectivity limitations and the SMHV direction-count loss are retained. Old source-role prose is replaced by context-dependent predictive interpretation; removing overstated prose does not remove adverse data.
